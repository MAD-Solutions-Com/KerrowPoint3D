Your game

How to play

Two players, each on their own device. One of you is in the lighthouse, the other in the harbour office, and you each see different things.

Talk to each other the whole time, on a call or across a table. Neither of you can solve anything alone.

One of you hosts and shares the code. Click things in the room to look closer. Your journal keeps what Mr Quick asked and what you have found. Ring Mr Quick on the telephone when you have an answer, or if you need a hint.

Your journal

Open it to see what Mr Quick wants, your progress and your clues. When you have an answer, ring him on the telephone.
