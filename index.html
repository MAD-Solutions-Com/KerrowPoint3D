How to play

Two players, each on their own device. One of you is in the lighthouse, the other in the harbour office, and you each see different things.

Talk to each other the whole time, on a call or across a table. Neither of you can solve anything alone.

One of you hosts and shares the code. Click things in the room to look closer. Your notebook keeps what Mr Quick is asking and what you have found, and Messages keeps everything he has said. Ring Mr Quick on the telephone when you have an answer, or if you need a hint.
